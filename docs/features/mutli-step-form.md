# Multistep Form

Multistep forms are a great way to break down long forms into smaller sections. This makes it easier for users to fill
out the form and reduces form abandonment.

## Create a Multistep Form

To create a multistep form, you need to:

1. Be inside the form builder. (Meaning you have already created a form or are in the process of creating one.)
2. Then in the "Form Structure" section, click the "Add Block" button.
3. A drawer will ope on the right side of the screen. Click on the "Page-Break Block" button.
4. A new page-break block will be added to the form structure. You can now drag and drop fields under the "Form Structure"
   section to manage on what page they should appear.
