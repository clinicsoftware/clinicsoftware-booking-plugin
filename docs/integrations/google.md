# Google Meet and Calendar Integration

This integration allows you to schedule Google Meet meetings and add them to your Google Calendar directly from your
form. This is useful for scheduling meetings with your form respondents.

Select between Google Meet or Google Calendar, when setting up the integration, and the form will automatically
schedule the meeting or add it to your calendar.

Then you can allow the user to set the reminder/meeting time and date, or you can set it up in the integration settings.

## How to use

To use this integration, you need to have a Google account and a Google Cloud project with the Google Calendar API, you
can follow this [tutorial](https://docs.simplecalendar.io/google-api-key/) on how to get them.

After that, go to "Settings" from the left menu under the "Integrations"
section complete with your client and secret.

Then inside the form builder select "Add to Google Calendar" or "Add to Google Meet" from Post Submission Action
